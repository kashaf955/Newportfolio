I design and ship full-stack products end to end — REST APIs, databases, and responsive, type-safe interfaces — with an emphasis on architecture that holds up across multiple clients and tenants as products grow.

Day to day, that means React and Next.js on the front end, Node.js and Express on the back end, MongoDB and Firebase for data, and enough AI tooling layered in to make the whole system a little smarter than a typical CRUD app.

Outside client work, I document and report for community initiative Dastgahe Karam, and previously led events and served as President at UEIT Society.
