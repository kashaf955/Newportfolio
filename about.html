Who's running this?

I'm Kashaf Tariq, a Full Stack Engineer who designs and ships products end to end — from database schema to the pixels a user actually clicks on.

I'm a full-stack engineer who enjoys turning ideas into complete, scalable products — from the initial architecture and database design to APIs, responsive interfaces, integrations, and deployment. I care about building systems that are not only functional today, but structured to remain reliable as users, clients, and requirements grow.

My work spans client projects, eCommerce platforms, SaaS applications, dashboards, and custom web solutions. I'm particularly interested in multi-tenant architecture, where a single system needs to serve different clients while keeping their data, workflows, and configurations properly isolated. I also explore AI as part of modern applications, using it where it can genuinely improve automation, decision-making, or the overall user experience — rather than simply adding AI for the sake of it.

I've worked across both frontend and backend development, which lets me look at products as complete systems rather than isolated features. I enjoy solving the problems between the layers — designing clean APIs, managing data and authentication, connecting third-party services, optimizing workflows, and creating interfaces that are practical and easy to use.

Beyond client and development work, I contribute to Dastgahe Karam, where I help document and report on community initiatives and their impact. I've also been involved in leading student communities and organizing technical events, including serving as President of the UEIT Society. These experiences have strengthened my ability to communicate, coordinate teams, take ownership, and turn ideas into something people can actually use.

At the core, I'm interested in building meaningful software — products that are thoughtfully engineered, solve real problems, and have the foundation to grow.

How I Work

A few things that stay consistent

01

Architecture before features

I map out how data will move through a system before I write the first route or component — especially on multi-tenant products, where a shortcut taken early becomes a bug for every client at once, not just one.

02

Questions before assumptions

I'd rather ask a client one clarifying question upfront than build the wrong thing well — a wrong assumption caught after launch costs everyone more than the two minutes it takes to ask.

03

Showing up outside client work

Outside client work, I document and report for community initiative Dastgahe Karam, and previously led events and served as President at UEIT Society — see the full community record.
