I design and ship full-stack products end to end — REST APIs, databases, and responsive, type-safe interfaces — with an emphasis on architecture that holds up across multiple clients and tenants as products grow.

Day to day, that means React and Next.js on the front end, Node.js and Express on the back end, MongoDB and Firebase for data, and enough AI tooling layered in to make the whole system a little smarter than a typical CRUD app.

I've built that range by working across different environments: a full-time engineering role building multi-tenant client software at DOSTSOL Global, a fellowship at Headstarter AI focused on shipping AI-powered products fast, and a shorter stint as a Web Developer at Hairium — each one adding a different way of thinking about the same underlying craft.

How I Work

A few things that stay consistent

Architecture before features

Before writing a route or a component, I map out how data will move through the system — especially for multi-tenant products, where a shortcut taken early becomes a bug for every client at once, not just one.

Type-safety as a default

I use TypeScript wherever the project allows it, not as a formality but because catching a shape mismatch at compile time is cheaper than catching it in a client's support ticket.

Showing up outside client work

I document and report for community initiative Dastgahe Karam, and previously led events and served as President at UEIT Society — see the full community record.
